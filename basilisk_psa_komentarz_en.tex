\chapter*{Introduction}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Introduction}

\textbf{English convenience translation.} This document is an English translation of the Polish commentary provided for convenience only. In the event of any discrepancy, the Polish version prevails. The document is not part of the Articles of Association.

The document is \textbf{informational}: it is not part of the Articles of Association, is not covered by the notarial deed or signature, and does not change the content of the Articles. It serves the founders, directors, future investors and their advisers to quickly locate the legal basis of each provision. In the event of any discrepancy, the text of the Articles of Association \Zr{us} prevails.

The document consists of two informational annexes:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Informational Annex A --- Bibliography}: national and EU legal acts, case law, literature, official materials and Company documents; the labels in square brackets are clickable;
  \item \textbf{Informational Annex B --- Legal Bases by Paragraph and Section}: for each section of the Articles, the provisions on which it is based, case law and a short comment. Paragraph numbers are clickable and open the Articles (\texttt{psa\_en.pdf} in the same directory).
\end{itemize}

Legal status: according to the consolidated text of the KSH (Journal of Laws of 2024, item 18, as amended) and the other acts listed in the bibliography. Provision numbers should be verified in ISAP before use in legal transactions; the holdings of judgments are given in summary form. The abbreviation ``PSA'' means a simple joint-stock company, ``GM'' --- the General Meeting.

\Par{firma}{Business Name, Registered Office and Duration}
\Ust{1}{Article~300\U{5}(1)(1) KSH \Zr{ksh}; Article~43\U{4} of the Civil Code (KC) \Zr{kc} (business name of a legal person); trade marks --- Article~120 et seq. of the Industrial Property Law (PWP) \Zr{pwp}}{The business name must contain the designation of the legal form (``prosta spółka akcyjna'' or ``P.S.A.''). \Ust{4}{Article~300\U{5} KSH (optional provision)}{The absence of a term means an indefinite duration.}
